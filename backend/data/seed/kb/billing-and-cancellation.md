# AcmeFlow Billing, Cancellation, and Refund Policy

> Example KB content for a portfolio demo, not a real legal or financial
> policy. This document explains AcmeFlow's billing rules in prose; whether a
> specific refund or ticket should actually be created for a given customer is
> decided by AcmeFlow's own eligibility checks and tools, never inferred from
> this text alone.

## Billing cycle and payment methods

Subscriptions are billed monthly, in advance, by credit card. An invoice is
generated at the start of each billing period and charged automatically to
the card on file. Failed payments are retried over 5 days before a subscription
is marked past due.

## Upgrading plans

Upgrading from Starter to Pro, or Pro to Enterprise, takes effect immediately
and is prorated: the customer is charged the difference for the remaining
days in the current billing period, then billed the new plan's full price
from the next cycle onward.

## Downgrading plans

A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing period, not
immediately — the customer keeps their current plan's features until then, so
they aren't cut off mid-cycle from something they already paid for. No
partial refund is issued for the unused portion of the higher plan.

## Cancellation

A customer can cancel at any time from the billing settings page. Cancellation
takes effect at the end of the current billing period; access continues until
then. Account data is retained for 30 days after the subscription ends, in
case the customer resubscribes, then permanently deleted.

## Refund policy

Refunds are evaluated case by case and are not automatic. Grounds that are
typically eligible for a refund: a billing error (e.g. being charged twice for
the same period, or charged after a cancellation request was already
submitted), or a service outage that materially prevented use of the product
for an extended period. Grounds that are typically NOT eligible: simply not
having used the product during a paid period, or changing your mind after
most of a billing period has already elapsed. A refund is never issued, and
never described to a customer as approved or in progress, unless an actual
refund tool call confirms it — this document describes policy, not a specific
decision.

## Billing disputes

If a customer disputes a charge they believe is incorrect, the right next
step is to escalate to a human agent with the specific invoice or date in
question, rather than guessing at an explanation — billing history isn't
something this document can look up for an individual account.
